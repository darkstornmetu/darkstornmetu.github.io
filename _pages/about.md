**Hi, I'm Buğra.**

I am a 33-year-old product manager/game developer and have been working at [Twenty Games](https://twenty.games), a mobile game company I co-founded, for the past four years. Before that, I worked as a solo developer for a year and a half. At Twenty Games, we developed hit games that have reached millions of users worldwide, most notably [*Makeup Kit*](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/makeup-kit/id1601985721) and [*Pasta Fever*](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pasta-fever/id1642532274).

While I started as a developer, over time I naturally gravitated toward product management. I've defined and prioritized product roadmap for a 10-person team, overseeing the full product lifecycle for 75+ Games. I’ve specialized in mobile game development as a whole—balancing core gameplay with monetization, retention, and engagement strategies. I’ve designed hybrid monetization models, implemented A/B tests, and created custom game economies that maximize both player value and business impact. With an economics background, I’ve always been data-driven and KPI-aware, especially when making feature decisions or shaping LiveOps content.

I still enjoy crafting gameplay that feels great and writing clean, easy-to-read code in Unity, but I now spend more time connecting the dots between development, data, and player experience. I actively contribute to game and level design and have developed internal tools to streamline the process. I’ve worked in Agile setups and collaborated closely with both creative and technical teams to bring ideas to life—fast. I’ve participated in the development of over 100 mobile games and learning new lessons from every project. Yet, I’m ambitious and believe this is just the tip of the iceberg—the best is yet to come!

Want to know more? Check out my projects, repositories, and resume at the top. You can also reach me through any of my contact channels.
